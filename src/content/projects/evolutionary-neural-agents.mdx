## Idea

Each agent is a small neural network that sees the world through seven ray sensors and outputs two
numbers: how much to turn and how fast to move. Nobody trains it with backpropagation. Every generation
the best agents are kept, crossed over and mutated, and the population slowly learns to reach the target.
